# Service illustrations

The owner requested AI or internet photography on 9 October 2026. These generated illustrations show service applications and equipment, not actual AQ Enterprises premises, staff, installations or customers. They are never used as verified project evidence.

- `home-security.webp`: AI-generated outdoor CCTV camera and house entrance.
- `access-control.webp`: AI-generated fingerprint attendance/access terminal.
- `video-intercom.webp`: AI-generated video door phone and monitor.
- `fire-alarm.webp`: AI-generated alarm call point, sounder and detector.
- `cctv-maintenance.webp`: AI-generated CCTV servicing workbench.
- `internet-connectivity.webp`: AI-generated router and optical network terminal in an office.
- `recorders-storage.webp`: AI-generated CCTV recorder, hard disk and camera equipment.
- `hero-security-connectivity.webp`: AI-generated mounted bullet CCTV camera and Wi-Fi router, used as the homepage equipment illustration (1440 × 1080, about 70 KB).

Files are compressed WebP assets and retain local Next.js image optimization. The owner can replace or hide them through Admin → Media library, or override a service image in its editor. Existing property photographs in `images/services` are illustrative stock from the source URLs recorded in `scripts/download-images.js`.
